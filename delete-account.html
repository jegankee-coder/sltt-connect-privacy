Delete your account & data

SLTT Connect accounts are created and managed by SLTT admin staff, not by self-signup — so account deletion works the same way: email us and an admin will action it. There's no separate password or extra login step needed for this request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email jdsignaturemobility@gmail.com from the address on file, or ask your SLTT admin directly.
  2. Tell us the name and phone number/email on the account you want deleted.
  3. An admin deactivates the account and removes its personal data, usually within a few business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, phone number, and email on file
  • Profile photo and any attendance/issue-report photos
  • Location/GPS history tied to your account
  • Face-verification (biometric) data, if you used attendance verification

What we may keep

Completed trip and billing records may be retained where SLTT is required to keep them for accounting or legal purposes, even after an account is deactivated — the same records any employer keeps for payroll/billing history. This doesn't include your live location, photos, or biometric data, which are removed.
